A preset opens as a frame beside the others, each with its own look, size and export. An image frame runs your picture through Lazuli's palette, steps, softness and grain. Only the selected frame animates. Try it: open a preset or an image from the bar, or drop an image anywhere on the canvas.

What a frame is

One look, one size, one export

A frame holds a full config (preset plus your changes), an aspect ratio and a source. The full-screen background today is just a single frame filling the window, so nothing breaks: <lazuli-bg> embeds one frame.

Image frames

Your picture, Lazuli's color

The image's brightness becomes the field that the palette maps onto, so Palette, Steps, Softness and Grain all apply. Here that's an SVG gradient map; in the engine it's the image as a texture feeding the same color pass the shapes use.

Performance and saving

One live frame at a time

Browsers allow only a few WebGL contexts, so unselected frames show a still render and only the selected one runs. A canvas of frames is a document worth keeping, which is where accounts come in later.
