What the pencil demo is made of
A lit surface, lines where it's dark, a sketchy outline and paper. Four ideas, stacked.
In Lazuli
Each idea is a layer you add and handle on the canvas: drag the sun, turn the lines. Restack them for other drawings.
Motion is a choice
The canvas starts still. Movement is a layer too: drift, breathe, or a light that follows the pointer.

Built from layers
